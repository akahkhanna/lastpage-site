Common questions

  • Where are the games? Tap "Last page" at the bottom right. The games are on the last page of the notebook.
  • How do I hide from the teacher? When you hear her footsteps, tap "Back to notes" or turn the page before the countdown ends.
  • I got caught. How do I get the games back? Do the punishment on the chit (lines, a times table, cleaning the blackboard…) or wait 10 minutes. The 5th catch in a day is a 10-minute detention. Your notes always stay usable.
  • Can I turn the teacher off? Yes: Settings → Teacher → Off.
  • Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your notebook.
