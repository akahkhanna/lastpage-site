Privacy Policy

That Last Page · Effective 16 November 2026

That Last Page has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and contains no third-party code that does any of these. Almost everything stays on your iPhone. The one exception is homework (below), which goes through a small server of ours so that it can reach the other person without a link; it is never linked to who you are.

What stays on your iPhone

Your notes and drawings, the name, class and school you write on the cover, friends' names you type into games, and your game results and teacher score are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

If you choose "send it" or "send it home", the app opens the standard iOS share sheet with a picture of the result. You decide where it goes. The app never sends messages itself and never reads your contacts.

Playing a friend on their own phone

From version 1.1, games with a friend on their own phone go through Apple's Game Center: Apple's servers connect the two phones and carry your moves (your name as it appears in the game, your moves, and in hangman the word you set). We never see them, and nothing is sent to us. Game Center is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Scores for the leaderboards and achievements are likewise held by Apple's Game Center under your Apple account. Inviting someone by sharing a link puts only the game's name and a random code in the link.

In version 1.0, "two phones" instead connected the two phones directly over the local Wi-Fi network (which is why that version asks for Local Network access); nothing went through any server.

Tests from the family, live games and the family board

These three go through a small server we run (on Cloudflare), so that they can reach the other person without a link. Each notebook makes up its own address (such as VIHAAN-7K2M), not tied to an account, a phone number, an email or an Apple ID; we have no way to connect it to a person.

  • Tests: the setter's and child's names as typed, the questions, the answers, the ticks and the remark. Deleted 30 days after the last change.
  • Live games (a friend on their own phone or in a browser): the players' names as typed and the moves of that game, kept in a "room" for a day after the last move so a phone that drops out can rejoin.
  • The family board: your daily puzzle times, against your address, for 90 days; a family (a name, its members' names and addresses) for 180 days after the last change.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, your phone's push token against your address, so a test or a game invite can knock.

Anyone with a test's or room's six-letter code, or a family's link, can see what's in it, so share them only with the people concerned. Tests can also be sent as a long link carrying everything inside it, in which case nothing is stored anywhere. Like any web host, Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs of connections (such as IP addresses); we don't receive or use them.

Motion sensor

The "stand on the bench" punishment uses the iPhone's motion sensor to check the phone is held still. This is processed on the device only while that screen is open and is not stored.

The game pages on this website

The FLAMES and "guess my word" pages on this website (for sharing a result or a challenge with someone who doesn't have the app) work entirely in your browser. The names, words and hints are kept in the part of the link after the "#", which browsers never send to the server, so we never see them. The challenge page remembers the name you type as the sender in your own browser, for next time; it never leaves your device. The pages use no cookies, analytics or trackers. They are hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, may keep standard technical logs of visits (such as IP addresses); we don't receive or use them.

Children

The app has no accounts and asks for nothing about anyone. What a child's use sends anywhere is what they choose to do with the family: a test paper they hand in, moves in a game with a family member, puzzle times for the family board, each under a made-up address and their first name as typed, through our server as described above; none of it is linked to the child's identity, and all of it is deleted on the schedules above.

Changes
