Privacy Policy

That Last Page · Effective 19 November 2026

That Last Page has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and contains no third-party code that does any of these. Your notebook stays on your iPhone. The exceptions are the things you do with family and friends (test papers, live games, the family board, puzzle challenges and notifications), which go through a small server of ours so they can reach the other person. None of it is linked to who you are.

What stays on your iPhone

Your notes and drawings, the name, class and school you write on the cover, friends' names you type into games, your game results and teacher score, and copies of your old test papers are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

If you choose "send it" or "send it home", the app opens the standard iOS share sheet (or WhatsApp) with the result or a link. You decide where it goes. The app never sends messages itself and never reads your contacts.

Playing a friend on their own phone or in a browser

Live games with a friend go through a "room" on our server (below): the players' names as typed and that game's moves. If you instead invite a Game Center friend, Apple's Game Center connects the phones and carries the moves; we don't see them, and Game Center is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Leaderboard scores and achievements are held by Game Center under your Apple account.

In version 1.0, "two phones" connected the two phones directly over the local Wi-Fi network (which is why that version asks for Local Network access); nothing went through any server.

What goes through our server

We run a small server on Cloudflare. Each notebook, and each browser, makes up its own address (such as VIHAAN-7K2M), not tied to an account, a phone number, an email or an Apple ID; we have no way to connect it to a person. If you join the same family under the same name on your iPhone and in a browser, the server notes that those addresses are one person, so a test or game reaches both.

  • Test papers: the setter's and child's names as typed, the questions, the answers, the ticks and the remark. Deleted 30 days after the last change.
  • Live games: the players' names as typed and the moves of that game, kept in a "room" for a day after the last move so a player who drops out can rejoin.
  • Families: the family's name, its members' names as typed and their addresses. Deleted 180 days after the last change, or straight away for you when you leave.
  • Your address's box: your daily puzzle times (for the family board), what's been sent to you (tests, game invites, puzzle challenges with the challenger's name and time) and, if you allow notifications, your iPhone's push token or your browser's push subscription (an address at your browser maker's push service). Deleted 60 days after it was last used.

Anyone with a test's or room's six-letter code, or a family's link, can see what's in it, so share them only with the people concerned. Tests can also be sent as a long link carrying everything inside it, in which case nothing is stored anywhere. Like any web host, Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs of connections (such as IP addresses); we don't receive or use them.

Motion sensor

The "stand on the bench" punishment uses the iPhone's motion sensor to check the phone is held still. This is processed on the device only while that screen is open and is not stored.

This website

The games on this website run in your browser. Playing the phone, FLAMES and today's puzzles stay in your browser entirely (except your puzzle times, if you're in a family, for its board); names, words and results in a shared link sit after the "#", which browsers never send to a server. Live games, test papers, the family board, challenges and notifications use our server as described above. Your browser keeps its address, the names you play and send under, your families and your puzzle results in its own storage on your device; clearing the site's data removes them. If you tap the bell, your browser asks your permission and then registers its push subscription with us, so a game, a test or a family member's puzzle time can notify you, and so can an evening reminder if you have a puzzle streak going; tap it again to turn that off. The pages use no cookies, analytics or trackers. They are hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, may keep standard technical logs of visits (such as IP addresses); we don't receive or use them.

Children

The app and website have no accounts and ask for nothing about anyone. What a child's use sends anywhere is what they choose to do with family and friends: a test paper they hand in, moves in a live game, puzzle times for the family board, each under a made-up address and their first name as typed, through our server as described above. None of it is linked to the child's identity, and all of it is deleted on the schedules above.
