Privacy Policy

Last Page · Effective 7 November 2026

Last Page does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party code that does any of these.

What stays on your iPhone

Your notes and drawings, the name, class and school you write on the cover, friends' names you type into games, and your game results and teacher score are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

If you choose "send it" or "send it home", the app opens the standard iOS share sheet with a picture of the result. You decide where it goes. The app never sends messages itself and never reads your contacts.

Motion sensor

The "stand on the bench" punishment uses the iPhone's motion sensor to check the phone is held still. This is processed on the device only while that screen is open and is not stored.

The game pages on this website

The FLAMES and "guess my word" pages on this website (for sharing a result or a challenge with someone who doesn't have the app) work entirely in your browser. The names, words and hints are kept in the part of the link after the "#", which browsers never send to the server, so we never see them. The challenge page remembers the name you type as the sender in your own browser, for next time; it never leaves your device. The pages use no cookies, analytics or trackers. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard technical logs of visits (such as IP addresses); we don't receive or use them.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
