Need help?

Email hello@thatlastpage.app and we'll get back to you.

On the website

  • Do I need the app? No. Every game runs in the browser: play the phone, or send a friend a link and play live. Friends with the iPhone app can play you from the app.
  • How do I play a friend? Pick a game, then "Play a friend, live" and send them the link on WhatsApp. They tap it and you're both in.
  • Why don't I get notifications? Tap the bell at the top. Links opened inside WhatsApp or Instagram can't notify: open the site in Chrome instead. On iPhone, add the site to your Home Screen first.
  • Someone's name or words weren't OK. Leave that family (family settings › Leave), and tell us with "Report a problem" on the family page, or email the address above with their name as shown and the family's code. We remove what's reported.
  • My family disappeared. Your family lives with this browser. If the site's data was cleared (or you're in a different browser), join again with the family's code under the same name, and you'll be back where you were.

In the iPhone app

  • Where are the games? Tap "Last page" at the bottom right. The games are on the last page of the notebook.
  • How do I hide from the teacher? When you hear her footsteps, tap "Back to notes" or turn the page before the countdown ends.
  • I got caught. How do I get the games back? Do the punishment on the chit (lines, a times table, cleaning the blackboard…) or wait 10 minutes. Your notes always stay usable.
  • Can I turn the teacher off? Yes: Settings → Teacher → Off.

Your data

Your notebook stays on your iPhone. Families, test papers, live games and puzzle times go through our small server so they can reach each other: there's no account, just the name you choose. Privacy →
